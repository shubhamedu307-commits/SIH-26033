## Problem Title

SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization

Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme

Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description

Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

##(**KISANSETU AI**)

#(**Connecting Farms. Empowering Farmers. Delivering Fair Prices.**)

KisanSetu AI is an AI-powered digital marketplace that directly connects farmers, FPOs, consumers and bulk buyers.

It aims to reduce unnecessary intermediaries, improve farmers' earnings and provide affordable agricultural products to consumers.

#(**Main features:**)

1. Direct farmer-to-consumer and farmer-to-buyer marketplace.

2. Farmer aggregation for fulfilling bulk orders.

3. AI-based demand forecasting and price analysis.

4. Smart logistics and route optimization.

5. Transparent pricing and digital payments.

6. Product quality verification and traceability.

#(**Innovation:**) Integrating AI predictions, farmer aggregation, buyer matching and logistics into one platform.

## Feasibility and Viability


The project is technically feasible using existing technologies and government agricultural datasets.

(**Challenges and solutions:**)

- Limited digital knowledge – Simple interface and regional languages.

- Poor internet – Lightweight, mobile-friendly application.

- Transportation costs – Shared logistics and route optimization.

- Inaccurate predictions – Historical data and model testing.

- Farmer participation – Partnerships with FPOs.


#(**Revenue model:**) Small transparent transaction fees, institutional buyer subscriptions and optional logistics services.
